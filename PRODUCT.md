# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Members**: adults and parents at a UK martial-arts club (Sport of Kings, Manchester). Parents manage child profiles, check kids into classes, track belt progress, pay memberships. Mostly on phones.
- **Instructors/coaches**: mark attendance at the mats, mostly on phones during or right after class.
- **Admins**: run the club — memberships, finances, events, attendance oversight. Split between phone at the gym and desktop for reviewing/reporting.

## Product Purpose

A club management platform: class scheduling, self and instructor check-in, attendance history, belt/grading progression, membership billing via Stripe, events, announcements, and donations. Success = members reliably checked in and billed, and staff able to see who's attending (by location, class, and member) without manual bookkeeping.

## Brand Personality

Premium, disciplined, warm. Gold (#C5A456) + dark green (#1B4332) on a light glassmorphism base; Inter/Outfit type. The club has an Islamic charitable ethos (Seerat Un Nabi, naseeha, Gift Aid donations) — the tone is respectful and community-minded, not corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS admin templates (endless identical stat-card grids, dashboards for their own sake).
- Dense data tables that require horizontal scrolling on a phone.
- Anything that looks like a gym-chain CRM — this is a community club, not a franchise.

## Design Principles

1. **Glanceable at the mats** — staff screens must answer "who's here, who's missing" in one look on a phone.
2. **One source of truth** — attendance, membership status, and payment state must never disagree between screens.
3. **Parent-first flows** — every member-facing action must work when acting on behalf of a child profile.
4. **Desktop for depth, mobile for action** — marking happens on phones; reviewing/reporting can assume more space.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigable, readable sizes, generous touch targets on marking flows. No formal compliance requirement.
